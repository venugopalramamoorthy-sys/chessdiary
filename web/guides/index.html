GUIDES

Practical, no-nonsense chess guides for club and tournament players who want to understand their games better — from reading a scoresheet correctly to the blunder patterns that cost the most rating points, plus guides to getting the most out of ChessDiary itself. Written by the ChessDiary team, drawing on patterns we see across thousands of imported games.

 GUIDE 02

Forks, back-rank mates, hanging pieces, and the in-between move — the handful of tactical motifs responsible for most lost games below master level.
 GUIDE 03

 GUIDE 06
PGN Import vs. Chess.com/Lichess Sync: Which to Use
The difference between uploading a single PGN and linking your account for bulk history sync, and when each one is the right call.
 GUIDE 07
How to Read a Stockfish Blunder Report
What quality labels, centipawn loss, and tactical motifs actually mean — and how to use them to study a loss instead of just skimming it.
