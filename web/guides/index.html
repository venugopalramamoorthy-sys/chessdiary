GUIDES

Practical chess guides for club and tournament players: tactics you can learn to recognise, endgame and pawn-structure fundamentals, ways to review and keep track of your own games, and guides to using ChessDiary itself. Where a guide shows a board position, the position and its claims have been checked against the Stockfish engine. Guides are published under the author's byline; see About for how they are made and how to report an error.
